The narrator cannot invent a number.

Guardrails that make a generated answer auditable

  • ✓Numeric allow-list. Post-processing rejects any figure not present in the engine envelope.
  • ✓Citation required. A claim without a resolvable local section is refused, not softened.
  • ✓Confidence floor. Low-confidence inputs produce a refusal with a reason and the missing data named.
  • ✓Append-only memory. Stored answers are replayable from the original envelope JSON.
