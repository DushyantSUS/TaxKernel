---
source: "ITA 2025"
section: "211"
title: "Installments of advance tax and due dates"
---
Under Section 211 of the Income Tax Act, advance tax on current income shall be payable in four installments: 15% on or before 15 June, 45% on or before 15 September, 75% on or before 15 December, and 100% on or before 15 March. Assessees opting for Section 44ADA may pay 100% on or before 15 March.
