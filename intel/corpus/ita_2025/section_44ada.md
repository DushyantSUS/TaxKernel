---
source: "ITA 2025"
section: "44ADA"
title: "Special provision for computing profits and gains of profession on presumptive basis"
---
Under Section 44ADA of the Income Tax Act, an eligible assessee engaged in any profession specified under Section 44AA(1) whose total gross receipts do not exceed ₹75 Lakhs (where cash receipts do not exceed 5% of gross receipts) can declare 50% of gross receipts as profits and gains from profession.
