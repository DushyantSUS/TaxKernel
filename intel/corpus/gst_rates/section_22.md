---
source: "CGST Act 2017"
section: "22"
title: "Persons liable for registration under GST"
---
Under Section 22 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act 2017, every supplier of services shall be liable to be registered under this Act in the State or Union territory from where he makes a taxable supply of services, if his aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds ₹20 Lakhs.
